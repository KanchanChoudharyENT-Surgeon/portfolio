## Executive Summary & Clinical Background

Dr. Kanchan Chaudhary is an ENT and Head & Neck surgeon with specialised expertise in **cochlear implant surgery**, **vertigo management**, **endoscopic ENT surgery**, **skull base surgery**, and **head & neck cancer surgery**. She currently practises as Consultant – ENT at Kauvery Hospital, Bengaluru (Electronic City and Marathahalli campuses).

She has assisted in over **120 cochlear implant surgeries** using the posterior tympanotomy approach and has independently performed posterior tympanotomy in **90 patients** — one of the most technically demanding steps of the procedure. Her experience spans the full cochlear implant pathway: audiological and paediatric evaluation, radiological work-up, speech and language assessment, surgery, and post-implant switch-on and mapping.

Before Kauvery, she was Assistant Professor in the ENT Department at Sri Aurobindo Hospital & PG Institute, Indore, where she helped run the cochlear implant and vertigo programmes and mentored postgraduate ENT trainees.

## Clinical & Surgical Expertise

### Core specialties

- **Cochlear Implant Surgery** — Hearing restoration for children and adults with severe-to-profound hearing loss; end-to-end care from candidacy to mapping.
- **Vertigo & Balance Disorders** — Patient-specific vestibular assessment and rehabilitation for dizziness, BPPV, and Ménière's disease.
- **Endoscopic Sinus & Nasal Surgery** — FESS, septoplasty, and rhinoplasty for chronic sinusitis, nasal blockage, and deviated septum.
- **Micro-Ear Surgery** — Endoscopic myringoplasty and mastoidectomy for tympanic membrane perforations and chronic ear infections.
- **Head & Neck and Skull Base Surgery** — Thyroid, salivary gland, and head & neck cancer surgery, including complex skull base procedures.
- **ENT Emergencies** — Airway management, tracheostomy, and neck exploration in acute and trauma settings.
